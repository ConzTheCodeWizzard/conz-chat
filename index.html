Conz
Founder & Lead Developer

Conz is the creator of ConzChat. His mission was — and still is — to build something genuinely fresh: a fast, private messenger that doesn't compromise.

  • Full Android app architecture & development (v1–v26+)
  • Go (Golang) backend — complete rewrite from Firebase
  • Real-time WebSocket messaging engine
  • Public & private group chat system
  • Read receipt system (S → D → R)
  • Voice notes, GIF support, in-app camera
  • Media drawer (Camera / Gallery / GIF)
  • Join/leave system messages with history cutoff
  • Profile pictures in DMs and group chats
  • Chat themes & UI/UX design
  • VPS deployment & server infrastructure
  • Notification sidecar & conversation persistence
  • This website
