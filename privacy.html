Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ConzChat ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the ConzChat mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use ConzChat, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, username, display name, and profile photo
  • Messages: Text messages, images, and voice messages you send and receive
  • Device information: FCM device token for push notifications
  • Usage data: Online status, last seen time, and typing indicators (these can be disabled in Settings)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the messaging service
  • To deliver push notifications for new messages and friend requests
  • To display your profile to other users
  • To enforce community guidelines and respond to reports

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage). Firebase is compliant with GDPR and other applicable data protection regulations. Your data is stored on Google's servers in accordance with Firebase's data processing terms.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase: For data storage and authentication
  • Agora: For real-time voice and video calls (only during active calls)
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect user safety

5. Messages and Content

Messages you send are stored in Firebase Firestore. Self-destruct messages are automatically deleted after the configured time period. You can delete your own messages at any time. Deleted messages are replaced with a deletion notice and cannot be recovered.

6. Voice and Video Calls

Voice and video calls are powered by Agora. During a call, audio and video streams are transmitted through Agora's servers. ConzChat does not record or store call audio or video. Please review Agora's Privacy Policy at agora.io for more information.

7. Push Notifications

We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications. Your device's FCM token is stored in our database and used only to deliver notifications to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile and account information
  • Your posts on ConzFeed
  • Your friend connections
  • Your Firebase Authentication account

Note: Messages you sent to others may remain visible as part of conversation history.

9. Children's Privacy

ConzChat is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Firebase with security rules that prevent unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us
